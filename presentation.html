Introduction

Insurance policies and "Terms & Conditions" documents are notoriously long, complex, and filled with legal jargon.

The Core Problem: Average users often agree to terms they don't fully understand, leading to surprises during claims.

Our project aims to bridge this gap using Artificial Intelligence to provide clear, actionable summaries.

Key Features

  • Domain-Adapted Summarization: Optimized for legal/insurance terminology.
  • Insurance-Specific NER: Automatically detects coverage, limits, and exclusions.
  • Risk Scoring: Categorizes clauses based on impact to the user.
  • Clause-Level Traceability: Every summary point is linked back to the original text.
  • Multi-Format Support: Handles PDF, Images (OCR), DOCX, and Text documents.

Methodology

The system follows a three-stage NLP pipeline:

1. Document Processing: Text extraction and OCR (Tesseract) for scanned documents.

2. Information Extraction: Named Entity Recognition (NER) to find key policy parameters.

3. Neural Summarization: Transformer models (BART) generate concise summaries.

Technology Stack

BART-Large-CNN spaCy FastAPI Streamlit Tesseract OCR Python-Docx Google Colab (T4 GPU)

Risk Scoring System

We classify clauses into four severity levels to help users focus on what matters most:

CRITICAL Clauses with major financial impact or exclusions.

HIGH Important conditions and policy limits.

MEDIUM Operational details and standard requirements.

LOW General information and definitions.

Future Scope

  • Database Integration: Storing document history for users.
  • Active Learning: Improving accuracy through user feedback loops.
  • Mobile Application: For on-the-go scanning of documents.
  • Multi-lingual Support: Translation of summaries into regional languages.

Conclusion

Our AI-Based Summarizer transforms dense legal jargon into user-friendly insights.

By highlighting risks and key entities, we empower policyholders to make informed decisions and reduce claim surprises.
